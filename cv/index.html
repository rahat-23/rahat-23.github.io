Research & Scholarship

A broad research programme across software and intelligent systems

Requirements Engineering remains a core research foundation, while current and emerging work extends into broader Software Engineering, AI and interdisciplinary intelligent systems.

Software Engineering

Requirements intelligence, software analytics & quality

Research includes natural-language requirements analysis, ambiguity detection, semantic grouping, traceability, prioritization, software-quality prediction and empirical evaluation of software data.

AI & Language Intelligence

NLP, machine learning and LLM-enabled systems

Work with semantic representations, classification, similarity analysis, language models and learning-based methods for text, software artefacts and intelligent decision support.

Trustworthy AI

Robustness, explainability and generalization

Research interests include model robustness, explainability, ablation, generalization testing, metamorphic testing, reproducible experimentation and failure analysis.

Applied Intelligent Systems

Healthcare, agriculture and operational decision support

Interdisciplinary work extends into medical imaging, agricultural AI and predictive decision-support settings where reliability and interpretation matter alongside predictive performance.
