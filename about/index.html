About

Research-informed teaching, applied AI and academic leadership

I am a Lecturer and Head of ICT at British International College, Krirk University, with a PhD in Computer Engineering from Chiang Mai University. My academic work brings together Software Engineering, Requirements Engineering, Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning, with a strong focus on practical, reproducible and explainable AI-assisted systems.

Academic Profile

Teaching grounded in computing practice

My teaching spans programming, object-oriented development, data structures and algorithms, computer networks, web development, information systems, data science and AI-related topics. I focus on helping students connect core concepts with implementation, problem solving and professional development workflows.

Research Identity

Software engineering enhanced by language and AI

My research investigates intelligent support for software requirements and software engineering, including requirements classification, semantic analysis, ambiguity detection, prioritization, refinement, traceability, conflict analysis, robustness and explainability.

Teaching Philosophy

Understand, apply, then build

I prefer structured learning that moves from concept to example to implementation. Students should not only know what a method does, but also why it works, where it is useful and how to apply it independently.

Research Approach

Practical, empirical and reproducible

I value clear problem formulation, transparent evaluation, reproducible workflows and research outputs that can be understood, tested and extended by other researchers and practitioners.
