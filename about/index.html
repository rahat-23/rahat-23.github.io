Academic Practice

How I approach teaching and research

Teaching Philosophy

Understand → apply → build

I prefer structured, practice-oriented teaching that connects concepts with examples, implementation and reflection. Students should understand not only how a technique works, but why it is used, what assumptions it makes and how to apply it independently.

Research Approach

Problem-driven, empirical and reproducible

I value clear research questions, transparent experimentation and evidence that can withstand scrutiny. Methods should fit the problem, results should be interpretable, and claims should remain aligned with the evidence produced.
