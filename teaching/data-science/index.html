Course Identity

Teaching students to move from raw data to defensible insight

The course introduces data science as an analytical process rather than a collection of isolated tools. Students examine how data is generated, identify quality problems, prepare datasets, perform exploratory analysis, evaluate statistical relationships and make evidence-based interpretations using real-world data.

Data Life Cycle Data Quality Missing Data Data Cleaning EDA Descriptive Statistics Visualization Correlation Hypothesis Testing Chi-Square T-Tests ANOVA Pandas NumPy
Learning Focus

Students are guided to:

  • Understand data types, data quality and the role of data in analytical decision making.
  • Clean and prepare imperfect real-world datasets for analysis.
  • Use exploratory analysis and visualization to identify patterns and relationships.
  • Interpret descriptive statistics, correlation and statistical test results.
  • Apply hypothesis testing to support evidence-based conclusions.
  • Use Python analytical libraries to organize, analyse and communicate data.
Teaching Approach

Understand → prepare → explore → test → communicate

Students work through an analytical workflow in which data quality, statistical reasoning, coding and interpretation are treated as connected parts of the same problem-solving process.

01 · Understand

Read the data context

Students begin by identifying what the data represents, how it was generated and what questions can reasonably be asked of it.

02 · Prepare

Improve data quality

Missing, inconsistent and invalid values are examined before analysis so students understand why preparation affects conclusions.

03 · Explore

Find patterns and relationships

Summary statistics, distributions and visualizations are used to build an evidence-based understanding of the dataset.

04 · Test

Evaluate statistical evidence

Hypotheses, p-values and appropriate statistical tests are used to move from observation toward analytical decision making.

05 · Communicate

Explain the result

Students interpret findings through visualizations, analytical explanations and dataset-based presentations rather than reporting numbers alone.

Curriculum Architecture

Course learning pathway

The public portfolio groups the course into analytical capabilities rather than reproducing internal schedules, grading or examination details.

01

Data Foundations & Quality

Establishing how data is created, represented and prepared before analysis begins.

  • Data Science and Data Analytics foundations
  • Data versus information versus insight
  • Types of data and the data life cycle
  • Real-world data generation
  • Missing, inconsistent, biased and noisy data
  • Cleaning and basic imputation strategies
02

Exploratory & Descriptive Analysis

Developing an initial understanding of a dataset through summaries, distributions and visual evidence.

  • Exploratory Data Analysis
  • Feature and target variables
  • Dataset summaries and distributions
  • Measures of central tendency
  • Measures of dispersion
  • Histograms, boxplots and univariate interpretation
03

Relationships & Statistical Evidence

Moving from descriptive patterns to questions about association and statistical significance.

  • Numerical versus numerical analysis
  • Scatter plots and correlation
  • Positive and negative relationships
  • Pearson correlation
  • Population versus sample
  • Null and alternative hypotheses
  • p-values, significance and Type I / Type II errors
04

Comparative Statistical Testing

Using appropriate tests to evaluate categorical relationships and differences between groups.

  • Contingency tables and expected frequencies
  • Chi-Square testing
  • One-sample t-tests
  • Independent t-tests
  • Paired t-tests
  • ANOVA and comparison of multiple groups
  • Statistical decision making and interpretation
05

Python Analytics & Integrated Data Projects

Bringing data preparation, exploration, statistical reasoning and communication together through practical analysis.

  • Pandas DataFrames and Series
  • NumPy foundations
  • Selection, filtering and cleaning with Python
  • Summary statistics and visual analysis
  • Dataset-based analytical workflows
  • Interpretation and presentation of findings
