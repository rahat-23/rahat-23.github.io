Artificial Intelligence & Internet of Things

An introductory course connecting foundational artificial-intelligence concepts with the architecture and behaviour of Internet of Things systems. Course Identity

Introducing intelligent and connected systems through foundations and simulation

The course develops a conceptual understanding of two rapidly evolving technology areas. On the AI side, students study algorithms, graphs, intelligent agents and problem-solving through search. On the IoT side, they examine connected devices, hardware roles, communication protocols, cloud-service concepts and simulated IoT environments.

AI Fundamentals Algorithms Graphs BFS & DFS Intelligent Agents Rationality Search Problems Uniform-Cost Search Greedy Search A* Search IoT Fundamentals Sensors & Actuators TinkerCAD IoT Protocols Cloud Architectures
Learning Focus

Students are guided to:

  • Explain foundational AI and IoT concepts using clear real-world examples.
  • Understand how intelligent agents interact with environments and make decisions.
  • Trace and compare major uninformed and informed search strategies.
  • Identify the roles of sensors, actuators, controllers and networks in IoT systems.
  • Use simulation to explore basic connected-system behaviour.
  • Relate AI and IoT concepts to practical smart-system applications.
Teaching Approach

Explain → reason → simulate → discuss

The course combines conceptual explanation with search-problem reasoning, simulation-based IoT practice, real-world examples and student-led discussion.

01 · Explain

Build the conceptual foundation

AI and IoT concepts are introduced through structured lectures, visual examples and familiar technology scenarios.

02 · Reason

Trace intelligent search

Students compare how search strategies explore graphs and how different decision rules change the path to a solution.

03 · Simulate

Model connected devices

IoT simulation activities allow students to explore device interactions and system behaviour without requiring physical hardware.

04 · Discuss

Connect ideas to real applications

Presentations, case discussions and collaborative activities help students explain how these technologies appear in real systems.

Curriculum Architecture

Course learning pathway

The public portfolio presents the course as a progression of concepts and applied learning rather than reproducing internal assessment schedules.

01

AI, Algorithms & Graphs

Establishing the computational ideas that support later search and problem-solving topics.

  • Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and IoT
  • Algorithms and computational problem solving
  • Graph concepts and graph types
  • Breadth-First Search
  • Depth-First Search
  • Real-world AI application examples
02

Intelligent Agents & Search

Understanding how an intelligent system represents a task and searches for a solution.

  • Agents and environments
  • Rational behaviour
  • Task environments and agent structure
  • Problem-solving agents
  • Uninformed and informed search strategies
  • Uniform-Cost, Greedy and A* search
03

IoT Foundations & Hardware

Moving from AI reasoning to the components and architecture of connected physical systems.

  • Introduction to the Internet of Things
  • Real-world IoT examples
  • Sensors and actuators
  • Controllers and connected devices
  • Basic IoT hardware concepts
  • System interaction and device roles
04

Simulation, Communication & Cloud

Exploring how IoT systems can be modelled, connected and understood within larger networked environments.

  • TinkerCAD-based IoT simulation
  • Design and testing of simulated IoT setups
  • Network and communication protocol concepts
  • Connected-system communication
  • Introduction to IoT cloud-service providers
  • Cloud architecture concepts for IoT
