Research

Software Engineering, AI & Intelligent Systems

Current & Emerging Directions

Research beyond a single problem type

Recent and ongoing work reflects a broader interest in how intelligent methods behave, generalize and create value across software and applied settings.

Requirements Intelligence

Semantic and language-aware software engineering

Developing methods for requirements classification, ambiguity analysis, refinement, similarity, prioritization and meaning preservation using linguistic, semantic and learning-based approaches.

Software Analytics

Prediction, evaluation and generalization in software data

Exploring machine-learning approaches for software artefacts and defect-related data, with particular interest in evaluation designs that move beyond convenient random splits toward more realistic generalization.

LLM-Enabled Software Engineering

From natural-language intent to reliable software behaviour

Investigating how requirements can be clarified, repaired or structured before downstream LLM-based software generation, with emphasis on semantic preservation, reliability and executable correctness.

Robust AI Systems

Testing model behaviour under realistic change

Studying whether AI systems remain dependable under altered inputs, distribution shifts and stress conditions using robustness analysis, metamorphic testing, ablation and failure-guided evaluation.

Application Domains

Intelligent systems across real-world contexts

I am interested in extending rigorous Software Engineering and AI methods into application domains where reliability, interpretation and decision support are particularly important.

Healthcare & Medical AI

Medical imaging and health-oriented intelligent systems

Research interests include computer vision, image enhancement, data-efficient learning and intelligent methods for analysing complex biomedical and healthcare-related problems.

Agriculture & Environmental Intelligence

Robust AI for variable field conditions

An emerging research direction includes robustness evaluation for plant-disease classification and broader intelligent systems designed for changing real-world agricultural environments.

Operational Decision Support

Prediction, explanation and actionable recommendations

Applied machine-learning work also extends to operational settings such as logistics and cold-chain risk, where predictive performance, explainability and recovery-oriented decision support must work together.

Research Principles

What connects my work

Reliability

I am interested in whether methods continue to work beyond convenient experimental settings, including new data conditions, stress cases and domain variation.

Interpretability

Strong performance is more useful when researchers and practitioners can understand the evidence, limitations and reasoning behind a system's behaviour.

Practical Impact

I value research that combines methodological rigor with problems that matter in Software Engineering and real-world intelligent-system applications.
