Research

Software Engineering, Requirements Engineering and AI

My research focuses on improving software requirements and software-engineering decision support through linguistic, semantic and machine-learning methods. I am particularly interested in approaches that are accurate, interpretable, reproducible and practical enough to support real engineering work.

Research Principles

What I value in research

Reproducibility

Clear data handling, documented workflows, transparent evaluation and enough methodological detail for results to be inspected and extended.

Meaning preservation

When improving natural-language requirements, better structure or readability should not come at the cost of changing the intended requirement.

Practical relevance

Research methods should be technically rigorous while remaining interpretable, usable and grounded in realistic software-engineering or AI settings.
